<h2>About Me</h2>
<p>
I am currently a Postdoctoral Research Fellow at the <a href="https://www.nus.edu.sg/">National University of Singapore (NUS)</a>, working with Prof. <a href="https://www.comp.nus.edu.sg/~whsu/">Wynne Hsu</a> and Prof. <a href="https://www.comp.nus.edu.sg/~leeml/">Mong-Li Lee</a>. I received my Ph.D. degree from <a href="https://www.sdu.edu.cn/">Shandong University</a>, under the supervision of Prof. <a href="https://liqiangnie.github.io/">Liqiang Nie</a> and Prof. <a href="https://xuemengsong.github.io/">Xuemeng Song</a>. From November 2022 to December 2023, I was a visiting Ph.D. student at the <a href="https://www.nextcenter.org/">NExT++ Lab</a>, NUS, supervised by Prof. <a href="https://www.comp.nus.edu.sg/cs/people/chuats/">Tat-Seng Chua</a> and Prof. <a href="https://weiyinwei.github.io/">Yinwei Wei</a>. My research interests lie in culturally aware multimodal large language models (MLLMs), multimedia computing, and information retrieval.
</p>
